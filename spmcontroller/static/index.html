Safety limits

The training board on the femur streams raw IMU data over USB. The laptop fuses it into the femur's orientation and angular velocity: tilt is absolute (gravity); heading (rotation about gravity) is gyro-only and is re-referenced to the encoders with Align.

Mount calibration

Rig must be homed and ARMED. The leg holds still at neutral, then rotates ±15° about the femoral head's x and y axes and ±25° about z, comparing the encoders with the gyro to find how the board is mounted. Saved to imu_cal.json (redo if the board is re-mounted).

Closed loop (optional)

IMU tilt trim1/s

0 = monitor only. > 0 integrates the IMU-measured tilt error into a correction (max 3°) of the motor command, to take out backlash/compliance the motor encoders can't see.
